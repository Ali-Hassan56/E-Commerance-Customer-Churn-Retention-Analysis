E-Commerce Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is a major challenge for e-commerce businesses. Losing existing customers can reduce repeat purchases and increase the need for costly customer acquisition.

This project uses SQL to analyze customer behavior, identify customers at risk of churn, compare active and churned customers, and identify opportunities for improving customer retention.

The goal is to turn customer data into actionable business insights that can support better retention strategies.

---

🔴 Business Problem

The e-commerce business is experiencing customer churn and needs to understand why customers become inactive or leave.

The business wants to identify:

- Customers who are at high risk of churn
- Customers who have made only one purchase
- High-value customers who may be vulnerable to churn
- Customers who have not purchased recently
- Customer segments with higher churn rates
- Behavioral differences between active and churned customers
- Early warning signs of customer churn
- Customers who should be prioritized for retention campaigns

Without this analysis, the business may lose valuable customers without knowing which customers require attention and why.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn using SQL
- Identify high-risk customers
- Analyze customer purchase behavior
- Compare active vs. churned customers
- Measure churn and retention across customer segments
- Identify early indicators of churn
- Create a customer retention priority list
- Provide insights that can support retention strategies

📊 Key Business Questions

1. Which customers have a high risk of churn?
2. Which customers have made only one purchase?
3. Who are the highest-value customers?
4. Which customers have not purchased recently?
5. Which customer segments have the highest churn rate?
6. How does the behavior of churned customers differ from active customers?
7. Which customers have high value but low purchase frequency?
8. Which customer segments have strong retention potential?
9. Which customers are showing early signs of churn?
10. Which customers should be prioritized for retention campaigns?

🔍 SQL Analysis

The project contains SQL queries designed to answer the above business questions and convert raw customer data into actionable insights.

The analysis focuses on key customer metrics such as:

- Churn
- Order Count
- Satisfaction Score
- Days Since Last Order
- Cashback Amount
- Preferred Order Category
- Customer Segment
- Customer Activity
